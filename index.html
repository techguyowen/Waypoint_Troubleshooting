📺 Embrace Conference Room TV & Video Extender

HDMI-over-Ethernet

The Embrace Conference Room TV receives its video feed via a dedicated HDMI-over-Ethernet extender system originating from the sound booth:

Transmitter (TX):
Located underneath the desk inside the main sound booth. Takes HDMI feed from the presentation/OBS broadcast computer.
Receiver (RX):
A small receiver box mounted directly behind the Embrace Room TV, connected to HDMI, Ethernet, and power.
Power Source (Shared Outlet Timer):
The receiver is powered by the same wall outlet timer controlling the room's Nest camera. If the timer is inactive, both devices lose power.

Troubleshooting "No Signal" on Embrace TV:

  1. 1
    Inspect HDMI Cable (Behind TV): Check the HDMI cable connected to the small receiver box behind the TV. Make sure both ends (receiver out to TV input) are fully seated, as this cable often gets bumped or unplugged.
  2. 2
    Check Power & Outlet Timer: Verify that the receiver box behind the TV has power lights on. Because the receiver shares an outlet timer with the Nest camera, ensure the timer is active and currently supplying power to the outlet.
  3. 3
    Inspect the Transmitter (Sound Booth): Check the transmitter unit located under the desk in the sound room. Confirm power, HDMI input (from computer/video feed), and Ethernet cables are securely connected and show an active link/power status.
  4. 4
    OBS Fullscreen Projection: Open OBS Studio ➔ Right-click video preview ➔ Fullscreen Projector (Preview) ➔ Select 'LONTIUM'.

💬 OBS Cloud Closed Captions Setup & Troubleshooting

Google Cloud Speech API

Real-time automated subtitles and closed captions for deaf / hard-of-hearing stream viewers and conference room screens.

  1. 1
    Open Plugin Menu: In the top OBS menu bar, click Tools ➔ Cloud Closed Captions.
  2. 2
    Turn On Captioning: In the Caption Preview dock, make sure the Captioning Enabled box is checked.
  3. 3
    Click Preview Box: Click the preview box inside the caption dock to make it turn on and start transcribing live voice audio.
  4. 4
    No Captions Generating? Verify active microphone is unmuted in OBS Audio Mixer, check ASIO Soundcraft soundboard USB driver connection, and verify internet connectivity.

🔊 House & Master LR Routing

  • 1
    Bus-to-Master Subgroups: Channels DO NOT directly feed Left/Right main outputs. They route to subgroup mix buses: Mix 12 (Stereo House) for non-drums and Mix 13 (Stereo Drums House).
  • 2
    'LR' Button Assignment: Mix 12 and Mix 13 are assigned to the main LR bus using the physical illuminated 'LR' button to the left of the console touchscreen.
  • 3
    Physical Main Output: The master stereo audio is physically sent via AES Out 01 digital output (NOT local analogue XLR 15/16).

🔉 Aux-Fed Subwoofer (Mix 8)

Low frequencies are isolated using Mix 8 (Sub) rather than an internal crossover.

✅ Allowed Sub Sources (7 Only):
1. Bass Guitar • 2. Keys • 3. Kick Drum • 4. Snare • 5. Low/Floor Tom • 6. Cajon/Stomp • 7. Synth Pad
❌ Excluded from Sub: Vocals, Acoustic Guitars, Speech mics, and Ambient room mics.

🔌 Stage Box vs. Local Preamp Split

  • 1
    Local Inputs (1–23): Standard floor stage boxes (Far Left, Front Left, Center Front, Front Right, Far Right, and Drum Left Plate) map 1:1 directly to local Si Impact analog preamps 1–23.
  • 2
    Drum Cage Digital Snake (MADI 17–24): The right-side drum cage wall plate bypasses local analog preamps and feeds directly into the Mini Stagebox 32i via the color-coded MADI snake (Brown, Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Purple, Grey).

1. Press MENU button next to touchscreen.
2. Tap SHOW ➔ Tap LOAD.
3. Use SCROLL encoder to highlight Sunday show file on MMC / USB.
4. Press illuminated APPLY button ➔ Confirm prompt.
⚠️ Protection Rule:
1NormalSunday is the church's master reference baseline. NEVER overwrite or save over 1NormalSunday. If creating custom settings for special events, always use SAVE AS with a new dated name.
