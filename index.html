📺 Embrace Conference Room TV & Video Extender

HDMI-over-Ethernet

The Embrace Conference Room TV receives its video feed via a dedicated HDMI-over-Ethernet extender system originating from the sound booth:

Transmitter (TX):
Located underneath the desk inside the main sound booth. Takes HDMI feed from the presentation/OBS broadcast computer.
Receiver (RX):
A small receiver box mounted directly behind the Embrace Room TV, connected to HDMI, Ethernet, and power.
Power Source (Shared Outlet Timer):
The receiver is powered by the same wall outlet timer controlling the room's Nest camera. If the timer is inactive, both devices lose power.

Troubleshooting "No Signal" on Embrace TV:

  1. 1
    Inspect HDMI Cable (Behind TV): Check the HDMI cable connected to the small receiver box behind the TV. Make sure both ends (receiver out to TV input) are fully seated, as this cable often gets bumped or unplugged.
  2. 2
    Check Power & Outlet Timer: Verify that the receiver box behind the TV has power lights on. Because the receiver shares an outlet timer with the Nest camera, ensure the timer is active and currently supplying power to the outlet.
  3. 3
    Inspect the Transmitter (Sound Booth): Check the transmitter unit located under the desk in the sound room. Confirm power, HDMI input (from computer/video feed), and Ethernet cables are securely connected and show an active link/power status.
  4. 4
    OBS Fullscreen Projection: Open OBS Studio ➔ Right-click video preview ➔ Fullscreen Projector (Preview) ➔ Select 'LONTIUM'.

Saturday Line Check & Rehearsal Workflow

Systematic Sound Engineering & Relational Worship Team Support

🎯 Target Gain: -8 to -10 dB
The Goal: To efficiently set the technical foundation for rehearsal while relationally supporting the worship team. A great line check is systematic, clear, encouraging, and creates a predictable rhythm every week.

🔌 1. Preparation & Strict Boot Order

Before Team Arrives

Follow the strict hardware boot order to protect speaker cones, amplifiers, and ensure digital sync:

⚡ Hardware Boot Order:
  1. 1
    Board (Console): Soundcraft Si Impact.
  2. 2
    Wireless Rack: Shure / Sennheiser receivers.
  3. 3
    ATLAS: Atlas sound processor unit.
  4. 4
    House AMP: Crown main house amplifier.
  5. 5
    QSC Wall Speakers: Active sanctuary wall speakers.
  6. 6
    All Video ATEM: Video switcher (only if there is an event).
  7. 7
    AVIOM Switch: Personal monitor network switch (located on left side of stage with bundle of ethernet cables).
Scene & Power: Load 1NormalSunday scene (Show ➔ Save As Date.show).
Confirm Roster: Review Planning Center services list to confirm who is playing and any stage changes.
Mute Foyer: Ensure foyer speaker feeds are muted on Layer A during setup.
PC & Talkback: Power on PC (only start Reaper if track recording). Prep talkback wireless mic (White/Blue Pastor mic on "Owens layer" with fresh battery).

🤝 2. The Arrival: Supporting the Team

How you welcome the team sets the tone for the entire rehearsal:

  • Greet by name: A personal greeting shows care and builds relational trust.
  • Help them settle in: Assist with direct boxes (located in the closet to the right of the stage), help find stage box ports, and ask "Do you have everything you need?".
⏱️ Click Track Patching Procedure (Console Layer B)
  1. Navigate to Layer B on the Soundcraft console.
  2. Press the SEL button above Fader 2 (labeled "Click").
  3. On the main soundboard touchscreen menu, press Inputs.
  4. Patch input source:
    • Front Click: FX1 ➔ recorder (or Mic 12 / Input 12 on Center Front Box).
    • Drum Cage Click: Mic 20 / Input 20 on Drum Cage Left Plate.
  5. Click Menu twice to exit.
💡 Practice Tip — Playing iPad Audio Through Aviom & House:
If someone wants to play reference tracks or rehearsal audio from an iPad/phone through both Aviom and House, plug the click cable into the iPad and connect to any spare instrument or vocal port on stage (e.g. Aux 1), then control on Layer A like normal. Advanced: You can also raise Click channel sends to Mix 12 (House).
⚠️ Service Caution: Use caution for live services—ensure Click sends to Mix 12 (House) are muted before Sunday service!

🎚️ 3. Systematic Line Check Process

Per-Musician Workflow

Use your wireless talkback mic for all communication. Work through each musician step-by-step:

1. Set the Gain (#1 Job): Using the channel gain knob (left of channel strip), dial signal to hit -8 to -10 dB on the meter (to the right of volume fader on Mixing Station). Gain impacts Aviom in-ears directly!
2. Tone & Presets: Mixing Station App: Select Channel ➔ Folder ➔ Local Presets. Copy/Paste: Folder ➔ Copy ➔ Paste.
⚠️ CAUTION: Double-check routing, gain, and phantom power (red +48V button) after pasting!
3. Tech Adjustments: Correct only noticeable EQ flaws to preserve preset consistency while honoring individual channel acoustics.
4. Confirm Aviom Signal: Ask: "Are you getting a good signal of yourself in your Aviom?" (Fixes personal mix issues early).
5. Set House Level: Bring their fader up in the main house mix to a starting position.
🥁 The Standard Order (Build Foundation First):
  1. Drums: Check each mic individually (kick, snare, toms) ➔ Tom balance check ➔ Simple steady beat for whole kit.
  2. Bass: Ensure sub-bass and house levels sound full and tight.
  3. Keyboards / Piano: Stereo width and patch level consistency.
  4. Violin / Special Instruments: Allow extra setup time and EQ work.
  5. Guitars: Acoustic clean signal ➔ Electric main rhythm + engage lead/boost pedals to test volume jump!
  6. Vocals: Background vocalists first ➔ Worship leader last (sing a line from a worship song, not "test 1, 2").

🎵 Rehearsal Phase (M.A.S.S. Protocol)

Once line check is complete, transition into full band rehearsal:

M — Mix the Band:
Blend instruments and vocals into a cohesive sanctuary mix.
A — Around Room:
Walk around sanctuary to check speaker coverage and balance.
S — Songs:
Learn arrangements, musical cues, and vocal dynamics.
S — Subs:
Check sub-bass performance and punch in the low end.
4. Final Check & Handoff: Ask "Everyone hearing what you need?". Use the first song to lock in FOH mix. Check wireless battery levels for Sunday (recharge above).
6. Hardware Shutdown Order (Exact Reverse):
AVIOM ➔ ATEM (if on) ➔ QSC (6 switches) ➔ House AMP ➔ ATLAS ➔ Wireless Rack ➔ Board
Note: Leave Aviom ON after set rehearsal if Sunday is next.

🚨 Fast Audio Fixes During Rehearsal

📢 Pastor Lapel Feedback: Pull down 2-4 dB & recall the Pastor Lapel channel preset in Mixing Station (Folder ➔ Local Presets). Do not randomly boost EQ bands!
🥁 Front Cajon Feedback: Must be MUTED whenever cajon is inactive. Mute directly on Layer A Fader 21 (note: it is grouped inside the Band Group).
🔊 Room Silent / Meters Bouncing: Venue Live 360 speaker box is off. Unplug and replug its power cable until it boots, and confirm Master LR red fader is up.
🎧 Aviom "Nothing in Ears": #1 failure is the headphone extender cable—plug earbuds directly into the Aviom unit to bypass. Check stage-left Aviom switch and Layer D masters.

iPad / Mobile Audio Playback (Playing Through Aviom & House)

Rehearsal song reference, practice tracks, and multi-destination routing

Practice & Rehearsal Workflow
⚠️ CAUTION FOR SUNDAY SERVICES (Rehearsal vs Live Service Rule): While routing playback through both the House and Avioms is ideal for Saturday practices and rehearsals, exercise caution during a live service! If you alter Click channel sends or patch through the Click line, ensure Click is NEVER routed to House (Mix 12 / Master LR) during a service so metronome clicks do not accidentally broadcast to the congregation.
🔌 Method 1: Repurpose Click Cable to Instrument Port (Recommended)

Quickest, safest, and most flexible method on stage:

  1. Take the 1/8" (3.5mm) click cable on stage and plug it into the iPad / phone adapter.
  2. Instead of plugging the other end into the click input, plug it into any spare instrument port (e.g. Aux 1 on Front Left Box / Input 3) or an unused vocal port.
  3. On the soundboard (Layer A) or Mixing Station app, navigate to that corresponding channel fader.
  4. Control the port like normal: push up the fader for House sound (Master LR / Mix 12), and raise channel sends for the band's Aviom in-ears.
🎛️ Method 2: Advanced Console Routing (Modify Click Channel Sends)

Route directly without moving stage cables:

  1. Keep the iPad plugged into the standard Click cable on Layer B, Fader 2 ("Click").
  2. On the console or Mixing Station app, select the Click channel and open its Sends / Bus Routing.
  3. Raise the send from the Click channel to Mix 12 (House) and uncheck/unmute any House routing restrictions.
  4. Audio will now play through both House speakers and Aviom in-ears simultaneously.
  5. ⚠️ Reset Before Service: Always set the Click channel's House send back to -∞ (OFF) before Sunday morning!
